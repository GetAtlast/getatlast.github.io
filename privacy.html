Privacy Policy

Atlast · Last updated November 21, 2026

Atlast is a time-tracking app for your habits. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: Atlast does not collect your data, and the developer cannot see it.

Information we collect

None. Atlast does not require an account and does not collect personal information, usage data, device identifiers, or analytics. It contains no advertising and no third-party tracking or analytics services.

Information you create in the app

The habits, sessions, notes, and other entries you create are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, this data is also synced through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) private database so it is available across your devices and can be restored if you reinstall the app. This data lives in your personal iCloud account and is governed by Apple's privacy practices. It is not accessible to the developer.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone. Because we do not receive your data, there is nothing for us to share.

Notifications and Live Activities

Atlast can send local notifications, such as a timer finishing or a habit reminder you set, and display Live Activities on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. These are generated on your device and do not involve sending data to us.

Exports

If you export your data, the file is created on your device. Where you save or share it is your choice.

Deleting your data

You can delete habits, sessions, and reviews within the app, and the deletion syncs to iCloud. Sessions recorded for a deleted habit are kept as uncategorized sessions until you delete them. To remove all data, delete the app and then remove Atlast's data from iCloud under Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Children

Atlast does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
