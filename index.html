Atlast

Time tracking for the habits you care about.

Atlast helps you see where your time really goes. Start a timer or stopwatch for a habit, log sessions after the fact, and watch your effort add up over days, weeks, and months.

What you can do

  • Track time with a timer or stopwatch, with pause, resume, and laps.
  • Follow a running session from the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island with Live Activities.
  • Log sessions manually, with optional notes.
  • See trends and breakdowns by habit and over time.
  • Export your data as CSV whenever you like.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking, and no analytics. Your data stays on your device and syncs through your own private iCloud, so the developer never sees it. Read the privacy policy.
