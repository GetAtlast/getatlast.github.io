Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

On your device, and synced through your private iCloud account so it follows you to a new phone or survives reinstalling the app. Atlast has no servers of its own, and the developer cannot see your data.

Sync isn't working. What should I check?

Make sure you are signed in to iCloud in Settings, that iCloud Drive is on, and that Atlast is allowed to use iCloud under Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Apps Using iCloud. Sync needs a network connection and can take a minute after you open the app.

My timer's Live Activity or notification isn't showing.

Check that Live Activities are enabled for Atlast under Settings → Atlast, and that notifications are allowed. Low Power Mode and Focus modes can also delay or hide them.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Atlast can export your sessions as CSV files from within the app.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting a habit or session in the app removes it from your device and from iCloud. To remove everything, delete the app and then remove Atlast's data under Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.
